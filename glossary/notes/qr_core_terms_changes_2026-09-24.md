## Substantive decisions and explicit clarifications

### 1. QR thesis and Thalion identity

The older compact transducer-theory formulation is retained under the newer relational-complexity thesis. Thalion, one thalion, and Thalean Transducer are unified as object/instance/function vocabulary for the G60 form, not renamed as G15 or a scalar.

Source basis: BASE, D6, SHAPE, CHAT.

### 2. Propagation event versus internal registered action

Repeated baseline event definitions are merged. The later manuscript limits the adjacent-handoff definition to the propagation sector; internal registrations are not automatically address-changing events. This is an explicit scope clarification, not a new physical event law.

Source basis: BASE, D6.

### 3. Complete input includes old memory

Recovery must reconstruct the incoming payload and relevant prior retained input. Finite local carrier plus unbounded active history is not a finite complete-state machine.

Source basis: BASE, D6.

### 4. Predictive equivalence requires the same future language

Agreement only on the intersection of common continuations can be vacuous. The revised entry requires equal admitted languages and equal responses, as specified by the later manuscript.

Source basis: BASE, D6.

### 5. Receipt and complete accounting

The shorthand "the receipt stays" is scoped to retention at the stated interface. A receipt need not be nontrivial when the onward output is already sufficient, and it need not remain at one physical location forever.

Source basis: BASE, D6.

### 6. Carrier triality is not the native G60 conjugation image

The baseline S3-type language is retained only as abstract Aut(V4). Native G60 fixes a and exchanges b/ab through C2. The separate registered-event S3 phase/Mode algebra is not conflated with that fact.

Source basis: BASE, ANALYZER, D6.

### 7. Delta and other repeated symbols

Registered distinction delta_e and history Delta[Gamma] are separated from Delta_1800. Sector M/Q, history Gram/Q_H/T_H, frame S/F, and multiple J/K/r symbols are similarly typed in the symbol register.

Source basis: BASE, CHAT, D6.

### 8. Selected reference, Phase, and Mode

Selected/executing reference, I-am-1 signing, incidence, isotropy comparison, and the actual C3-semidirect-C2 Mode/Phase action are added. These do not manufacture a missing native coupling.

Source basis: D6, REFCHAT, SHAPE.

### 9. Freedom has two different measures

Degree/rank-based permissible-action freedom is separated from finite-completion log-count. Carry-forward and opportunity expenditure are preserved as context-specific working constructions. Neither is identified with time or complexity.

Source basis: FREEDOM, CONSOL.

### 10. History growth is not universal scalar growth

An appended occurrence changes the history record. This does not prove every action increases time winding, complexity, spectral entropy, or loss of freedom.

Source basis: BASE, FREEDOM, D6.

### 11. Informative action, coupling, current, and backaction

These roles are indexed separately. The pair-envelope actuator remains a reported informative-dependence example, not the missing Born source or an executable native specification.

Source basis: D6, BORNCHAT.

### 12. Real reflection versus signed Pauli observable

O_i=2Pi_i-I_3 is unchanged by n->-n; A(n)=n dot sigma on C^2 changes sign. Both appeared near the analyzer discussion and must not inherit each other's sign laws or tensor capacities.

Source basis: ANALYZER, EPR, D6.

### 13. Raw sheet grade versus sheet-exchange eigenvalue

b/ab labels and Z_hist are distinct from q, the eigenvalue of Q_H. The four rank-three spectral cells are not literal three-point subsets or raw receipt-sheet labels.

Source basis: HIST, CLIFF, CHAT.

### 14. Preserve, but do not newly certify, the latest full-action claims

The native twelve-pentagon/six-axis common-domain and cocycle-cancellation realization are retained as reported advances. Their separate producer/action tables were not recovered for this update. Source-backed Gram data and the conditional cocycle algebra retain their stronger appropriate evidentiary roles.

Source basis: CHAT, HIST, TRANSPORT, CONSOL.

### 15. Current-square scope

The exact C5 identity, spectrum, and normalization are archived. Restricted current uniqueness assumes scalar coefficients and an internal spectator. Spectral equal splitting does not eliminate the separate chronological two-toggle or derive the general physical Born rule.

Source basis: CHAT.

### 16. No cardinality or carrier shortcut

7,200-dimensional harmonic space is not a proven 7,200-element receipt set. Five frame labels are not identified with analyzer axes. This missing identification is not rewritten as an impossibility theorem for every richer bridge.

Source basis: CHAT, G9000.

### 17. Bell witness versus operator capacity

1+3/sqrt(5) in the ideal singlet setting table is kept distinct from 2sqrt(41)/5 for the real-three-dimensional reflection operator. No-signaling of the table is kept distinct from a physically separated implementation.

Source basis: EPR, D6, CHAT.

### 18. Balanced release and thermal scope

Balanced release is the current conversion principle, not equality of retained and transmitted amounts. Arbitrary eigenmode occupancy is not automatically thermal. The hbar registration-scale wording remains a conjecture, with no numerical physical calibration.

Source basis: D6, STANDING.

Date: 2026-09-24

- QR-162 defines the **Thalean magnitude class** `J_Th`.
- QR-148 now also records the **Thalean magnitude-square measure candidate** `rho_Th`.

Key distinction:

```text
current = orientation x magnitude
-D_Th = reversed orientation at unchanged magnitude
rho_Th = normalized magnitude squared
```
