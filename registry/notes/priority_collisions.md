## Four archival decisions

**Consolidate an entity name** only when the source supplies its identity. For example, the source identifies census child G6 with native G60; retain the census view and the actual quotient map.

**Link equivalent views** when there is a specified action map, such as faithful rooted descent between K120, G60 and G15. Preserve their different ambient domains.

**Link a construction without treating it as an alias**, such as T10 generating a C10 subgroup, a quotient erasing a deck coordinate, or a matrix being normalized to an involution.

**Hold a proposed identification** when only group type, counts, spectra or a secondary locator match. The missing datum is the original action, embedding, map or provenance, not a more persuasive name.
