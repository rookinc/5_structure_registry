# Quantum Relativity / Thalean Core Terms

Status: Consolidated working glossary and theorem-boundary index  
Date: 2026-09-24  
Scope: Quantum Relativity, Thalean graph theory, event primacy, registered history, freedom, measure, Born/EPR, and current G9000 bridge language.

This file is a deduplicated replacement candidate for `notes/qr_core_terms.md`.

It consolidates the existing core-terms note with the QR / Thalean work developed through 24 September 2026. It is intentionally conservative about theorem status. A term may be stable as vocabulary even when its proposed physical interpretation remains open.

The primary editorial rule is:

> One canonical definition per term. Specialized uses refine the canonical definition; they do not silently redefine it.

A second rule is:

> Matching numbers, dimensions, phase arithmetic, or group orders do not identify carriers. A native identification requires a map, common action domain, intertwiner, or exact crosswalk.

---

# Status key

The glossary uses the following status labels where useful.

- **D - Definition.** Vocabulary or notation adopted by the QR / Thalean program.
- **P - Proved finite structure.** Exact finite combinatorics, algebra, group action, or theorem on a declared carrier.
- **X - Proved crosswalk.** Two independently defined objects are joined by an explicit map, conjugacy, common-domain comparison, or intertwiner.
- **C - Exact conditional construction.** Exact once a declared representation, registration, orientation, or control choice is supplied.
- **H - Working hypothesis / candidate.** Structurally motivated but not yet natively forced.
- **F - Physical correspondence open.** A finite object exists, but identification with laboratory physics is not established.
- **R - Rejected identification / guardrail.** An attractive conflation has been tested, ruled out, or explicitly forbidden.

Status attaches to a claim on a domain. It does not attach permanently to a word. For example, "sheet" is a definition, while identifying a particular sheet with a physical degree of freedom would be a separate physical claim.

# Part I - Identity and scope

## Quantum Relativity

**Status: D/H**

Quantum Relativity, or QR, is a theory of relational complexity governed by conservation of information.

Its primitive subject is not an isolated object moving through a pre-existing background. Its primitive subject is a registered relational object together with the admissible transductions by which its distinctions can propagate, remain retained, become comparable, and acquire history.

The short working statement remains:

> Quantum Relativity is transducer theory.

A fuller statement is:

> QR studies how relational possibility becomes registered actuality through information-preserving admissible transduction, and how causal, historical, quantum-like, and geometric readouts may arise from that common event architecture.

QR currently treats the following as distinct theorem fronts:

1. relational carrier structure;
2. admissible local action;
3. conservation / recoverability;
4. propagation;
5. retained receipt;
6. registered history;
7. orientation / incidence reconciliation;
8. measure;
9. frequency realization;
10. physical correspondence.

QR does not currently claim that all ten fronts have been reduced to one completed physical theory.

## QR transducer

**Status: D**

A QR transducer is a local relational system that:

1. receives a complete incoming relational state;
2. determines which continuations are admissible in the declared context;
3. realizes an admissible transformation;
4. separates what can propagate from what must remain retained;
5. hands the propagatable component to an adjacent relation;
6. retains enough complementary distinction to account for the completed event.

Write schematically:

    T : x -> (p,r)

where:

    x = complete incoming state
    p = propagated component
    r = retained receipt.

The recoverability condition is imposed on the complete admissible output, not necessarily on either branch alone.

## History-bearing transducer

**Status: D/H**

A history-bearing transducer is a finite relational transducer whose present admissibility or response may depend on retained distinctions generated by prior admissible events.

Its architecture is:

    finite carrier
    +

    +
    retained history.

A history-bearing transducer need not have an unbounded instantaneous carrier.

Where unboundedness is required, it may reside in extensible registered history rather than in the finite local state set.

A retained distinction counts as dynamically relevant history only when it can affect a later admissible distinction or response.

## Relational complexity

**Status: D/H**

Relational complexity is the nonnegative quantity intended to measure **how much relational organization is present in a declared registered object**.

A useful abstract type is:

    C : R / ~=_reg -> R_{\ge 0},

where `~=_reg` identifies equivalent registered presentations on the declared domain.

The essential content is not the notation but the typing:

- the domain must be stated;
- the registration level must be stated;
- equivalent presentations should not change the value;
- the transformation behavior must be stated;
- the normalization must be stated.

Relational complexity is not raw vertex count, raw path length, phase, Mode, time, probability, or freedom.

Two relationally different objects may have the same scalar complexity.

The current program does **not** yet possess one universal native formula for relational complexity.

Optional scalar encoding used in manuscript work:

    S(R) = G^{C(R)},    G > 1.

This is an encoding, not the definition of complexity itself.

Short form:

> Relational complexity asks "how much relational organization?"

This does not deny regularity.

Thus:

    law
    =

Today's stronger working phrasing is:

> Law resides in permissible action.

A law constrains what can happen. It is not itself the event that happens.

# Part II - Event architecture

## Relation

**Status: D**

A relation is a locally available relational connection between distinguishable states, interfaces, or transducers through which an event could occur.

Short form:

    relation = available relational possibility.

A relation is not yet a transmission and not yet an event.

The carrier may supply adjacency, but admissibility can depend on additional registered context.

A relation instantiates an action space. Without a declared relation, there is no relationally instantiated action space for that interaction.

---

## Possibility

**Status: D**

A possibility is a continuation compatible with the currently available relation before realization.

Three categories must remain distinct:

    possible
    actual
    impossible.

"Possible" means admitted by the current relational constraints.

"Actual" means realized as an event.

"Impossible" means excluded by the relevant admissibility constraints.

Possible does not mean destined.

Actual does not mean retrospectively necessary.

---

## Impossible continuation

**Status: D**

An impossible continuation is a candidate next relation or action excluded by the native constraints active in the declared relational context.

An impossible continuation is not merely one that failed to occur.

This distinction is essential:

    unchosen possible
    !=
    impossible.

---

## Admissibility

**Status: D**

Admissibility is the property of satisfying the native relational constraints required for a continuation or transduction to be lawful.

Admissibility is relational and context-typed.

It may depend on:

- current carrier state;
- relation;
- retained history;
- registration;
- selected reference or orientation presentation;
- declared apparatus constraints.

Changing coordinates or reference should not manufacture admissibility. It may change the presentation of the same admissibility relation.

Short form:

    admissibility = relational permission.

Admissibility does not itself designate which permitted event will be realized.

---

## Continuation

**Status: D**

A continuation is a lawful possible next relation available from the current relational and registration context.

Thus:

    relation
    =
    available relational possibility

    continuation
    =
    lawful possible next relation

    realized continuation
    =
    action / event.

A continuation remains possibility until it is realized.

---

## Admissible action

**Status: D**

An admissible action is a realized change permitted by the active relational constraints.

The action is real when it occurs.

The set of admissible actions can exist before any one of them is realized.

A selected reference may coordinatize an admissible action but does not create the freedom represented by the action.

A useful QR distinction is:

    permission
    !=
    designation
    !=
    realization.

Geometry or carrier structure may determine permission.

Additional relational structure may constrain or designate among permitted alternatives.

The event is the realized action.

---

## Admissible transduction

**Status: D**

An admissible transduction is a local transformation of the complete incoming relational state that:

1. satisfies the native relational constraints;
2. preserves the distinctions required for a legitimate handoff;
3. separates propagatable consequence from retained consequence;
4. remains recoverable from the complete output on the admitted image.

Schematic form:

    complete incoming state
    ->
    propagated component + retained receipt.

"Admissible" is stronger than "algebraically definable."

An algebraic map can exist without being part of the native transducer grammar.

---

## Informative action

**Status: D**

Informative action is an admissible realized continuation that creates, changes, or preserves a registered relational distinction in a way that can matter to subsequent accounting.

Schematic form:

    x
    ->
    admissible realized action
    ->
    (propagated consequence, retained consequence).

Action is more primitive here than a continuum action functional.

It means actualized relational becoming.

Short keeper:

> Action is always real.

The law may describe possibilities. The event records an action that actually occurred.

---

## Propagated component

**Status: D**

The propagated component is the part of the transduced relational state that can acquire the next admissible field address while preserving admissibility.

It is what crosses the adjacent handoff.

Short form:

    propagated component = what continues.

The propagated component need not by itself contain enough information to reconstruct the incoming state.

Recoverability belongs to the complete output.

---

## Trace

**Status: D**

A trace is a retained relational consequence of informative action.

A trace is the broad term.

Not every trace is automatically sufficient to account for an admissible handoff.

---

## Retained receipt

**Status: D**

A retained receipt is a trace that preserves the complementary information required to distinguish, attest, compare, or reconstruct a completed admissible transduction.

Short form:

    receipt = retained accounting distinction.

A receipt is not discarded information.

It is part of the complete output of becoming.

The receipt may remain locally stored, relationally distributed, or represented in a history carrier, depending on the construction.

---

## Sigma

**Status: D/C**

`Sigma` denotes a relational comparison or encounter operation/interface in the Thalean program.

Sigma is the place or operation through which relational distinctions are compared or exposed.

Sigma is not itself the residue left by the comparison.

Guardrail:

    Sigma != RSR.

## Relational Spin Residue / RSR

**Status: D/C/F**

Relational Spin Residue, or RSR, is the nontrivial central or projective residue retained by a lifted oriented relational composition after the corresponding visible relation has closed, commuted, or become indistinguishable under projection.

Short form:

> RSR is the spin-sensitive difference that survives relational closure.

RSR is a finite relational term.

It is not identified with physical spin without a physical embedding theorem.

## Adjacent handoff

**Status: D**

An adjacent handoff is the completed transfer of the propagated component across exactly one admissible neighboring relationship.

It is the primitive local unit of causal propagation in the current `c_Th` normalization.

The handoff is not complete unless the required retained information is also accounted for.

## Event

**Status: D**

A QR event is one completed, receipted, admissible relational handoff that has enough registration to be located in history.

Canonical form:

    available relation
    ->
    admissible continuation
    ->
    local transduction
    ->
    propagated component + retained receipt
    ->
    adjacent handoff
    ->
    registered event.

An event therefore includes:

1. a realized admissible action;
2. propagation or local consequence;
3. retained distinction;
4. completion / registration sufficient for history.

An event converts:

    available relational possibility
    ->
    attested relational actuality.

A realized event is singular.

Plurality before registration belongs to admissible alternatives, not to multiple simultaneously realized classical events unless a separate construction explicitly says otherwise.

## Event primacy

**Status: D/H**

Event primacy is the QR principle that physical becoming is fundamentally represented by completed relational events, while states are representations of conditions before, after, or across those events.

The primitive description is not merely:

    state_n -> state_(n+1).

It is:

    relation
    ->
    admissible action
    ->
    transduction
    ->
    propagation + receipt
    ->
    completed event.

States remain useful.

Event primacy says the event grammar carries the causal and historical meaning.

---

## Becoming

**Status: D**

Becoming is the ordered composition of completed receipted relational events.

For events `E_1,...,E_n`:

    Gamma = E_n o ... o E_2 o E_1.

One primitive unit of becoming is one completed event.

Becoming does not require an external time parameter to exist first.

Registered becoming supplies the history from which Thalean time is read.

---

## Registered history

**Status: D**

Registered history is the ordered compositional record of realized relational events together with the retained distinctions that keep those events distinguishable.

Short forms:

    history = attested actuality

and:

    history = retained compositional record of becoming.

History is richer than an endpoint list.

Two executions can have the same visible endpoint and different registered histories.

Today's correction should be treated as canonical:

> History accumulates with every realized action.

Freedom does not necessarily diminish with every action. History does.

---

## Complete QR state

**Status: D/H**

A complete QR state contains:

    present relational configuration
    +
    all retained distinctions relevant to future admissible response.

The criterion for retaining history as part of effective state is operational.

A retained distinction is relevant when there exists a common admissible future continuation that can respond differently depending on that distinction.

Therefore:

    same visible endpoint
    !=
    necessarily same complete QR state.

This criterion prevents arbitrary inaccessible details of the past from being promoted to physical state merely because they can be written in a log.

---

## Predictive history

**Status: D/H**

Predictive history is retained history with future discriminating power.

Two histories `H_1` and `H_2` are predictively equivalent on a declared continuation family when every relevant common admissible continuation produces the same registered response from both histories.

Write schematically:

    H_1 ~_pred H_2

iff all relevant common future tests agree.

This is the operative QR distinction between:

    passive archive

and:

    retained state-relevant history.

---

## Visible return

**Status: D/P**

Visible return occurs when a projected, quotient, or otherwise reduced state returns to a previously observed value.

Visible return concerns the chosen presentation.

## Complete return

**Status: D/P**

Complete return requires the complete retained relational state on the declared carrier to recur.

Therefore:

    visible return
    !=
    complete return.

The G900 / G1800 and registered-history constructions provide exact finite examples of this separation.

# Part III - Information, registration, and closure

## Thalean conservation of information

**Status: D/H**

Thalean conservation of information states that an admissible event may redistribute relational information but may not destroy the distinction structure required to account for the completed transduction.

Primitive form:

    incoming relational state
    ->
    propagated component + retained receipt.

The fundamental requirement is **recoverability**, not an assumed scalar additive conservation law.

Thus the strongest primitive statement is:

> Admissible becoming may redistribute relational information between propagation and receipt, but may not erase the distinction structure required to reconstruct the completed handoff.

If a future additive information functional is derived, it may decompose into propagated, retained, and correlation terms. Such an additive formula is downstream of recoverability.

Today's freedom correction follows directly:

> Freedom can be spent. Information cannot be discarded.

Loss of future options does not require loss of information about how those options were resolved.

---

## Recoverability

**Status: D/P on declared constructions**

Recoverability is the requirement that the complete input can be reconstructed from the complete admissible output on the admitted image.

For:

    T : x -> (p,r),

recoverability means:

    x = T^{-1}(p,r)

on the admitted image.

This does not require `p` alone or `r` alone to be invertible.

Recoverability is the most primitive operational content of QR information conservation.

---

## Registration

**Status: D**

Registration is the process by which a realized relational distinction becomes represented in a stable readout, receipt, or history domain.

Registration answers:

> What became recorded as having happened?

Registration is downstream of admissibility and event realization.

Registration is not the same thing as selecting which event occurs.

Guardrail:

    registration != selection.

---

## Registered distinction

**Status: D**

A registered distinction is a difference retained by becoming strongly enough to remain available to history or later comparison.

For one event:

    delta_e = local registered distinction.

For a history `Gamma`:

    Delta[Gamma] = accumulated ordered registered distinction.

Structural sequence:

    event
    ->
    delta_e
    ->
    Delta[Gamma]
    ->
    possible scalar or temporal readout.

Guardrail:

`Delta[Gamma]` is not `Delta_1800`, the G1800 involution.

---

## Registration nonamplification principle

**Status: D/P as information principle**

A faithful downstream registration process cannot create a distinction absent from the upstream instrument output.

If two upstream alternatives are identical with respect to all information available to the registration transducer, a faithful registration cannot make them distinguishable without introducing an additional transduction.

Thus:

    registration cannot repair a weak instrument.

And:

    measure-changing registration
    !=
    faithful registration.

---

## Closure

**Status: D**

Closure is the operation by which accumulated relational history is brought into a common comparison context so that a distinction can become registered or tested.

Schematic form:

    lifted / oriented history
    ->
    common comparison / closure
    ->
    registered distinction.

Closure does not necessarily restore the complete prior history.

Therefore:

    relational closure
    !=
    historical return.

---

## Closure residue

**Status: D**

A closure residue is the retained relational distinction remaining after histories or actions have been brought into a common comparison context.

Schematic form:

    rich history
    ->
    closure
    ->
    residue.

A closure residue may become part of a receipt.

RSR is one specialized spin-sensitive form of retained closure residue.

No universal identification is made between residues from different constructions without a crosswalk.

---

## Closure feedback

**Status: H**

Closure feedback is the dependence of later admissibility on a residue retained from an earlier closure.

If `r_n` is a retained closure residue:

    A_{n+1} = A_{n+1}(r_n)

schematically means the next admissible continuation family depends on that residue.

Closure feedback is not assumed universally.

It exists only when a native mechanism actually couples the retained residue to future admissibility.

# Part IV - Reference, incidence, phase, and orientation

## Selected reference

**Status: D**

A selected reference is a declared relational datum used to coordinatize a presentation, comparison, or execution.

Selecting a reference:

- fixes a presentation convention;
- can orient a torsor;
- can define local positive / negative labels;
- can make a relational account readable.

Selecting a reference does **not**:

- privilege one physical frame;
- create a missing relation;
- create freedom;
- manufacture a common incidence domain;
- repair incompatible transport laws.

Canonical formulation:

> GR says no frame is privileged. QR says: agreed. Select one. Then we can start.

"Select" is the preferred verb.

---

## Executing reference

**Status: D**

An executing reference is the selected reference together with the declared registration interface, retained context, and orientation information actually used by an execution or readout.

It is an operational datum.

It is not a privileged physical center.

The executing reference is where the account is situated.

---

## "I am 1"

**Status: D**

"I am 1" is a typed reference normalization on a two-member orientation torsor.

It means, schematically:

    the selected local reference member is named +1.

It does not mean:

- the observer is the scalar number one;
- the operator is the identity;
- relational complexity equals one;
- `c_Th = 1` has been rederived;
- the selected frame is physically privileged.

It is a presentation convention that permits relational comparison to begin.

---

## Situated / subjective account

**Status: D**

A situated account is a relational account expressed from a selected record-bearing reference.

In the current QR vocabulary, "subjective" means **situated**.

It does not mean:

- inaccurate;
- unsigned;
- imaginary;
- physically privileged;
- disconnected from objective comparison.

Objectivity is supplied by covariance and consistent relational comparison between situated accounts.

---

## Incidence

**Status: D**

Incidence is the intermediate relation through which two relational presentations, objects, histories, or orientation carriers actually meet.

Incidence is where law becomes executable between otherwise separately defined objects.

Useful keeper:

> No path without incidence.

A common number of states or an abstract group isomorphism does not create an incidence.

A common domain must be constructed.

---

## Signed incidence

**Status: D/P on declared incidence carriers**

Signed incidence is incidence equipped with orientation comparison data.

It permits an oriented history and an oriented boundary / edge / analyzer presentation to be compared without assigning contradictory intrinsic signs to the underlying state.

A state may therefore carry opposite signed boundary presentations at different incidences without contradiction.

Shared-incidence products can preserve relative agreement while cancelling arbitrary local sign conventions.

---

## Orientation torsor

**Status: D**

An orientation torsor is a two-member set carrying a free transitive `C2` sign action but no intrinsically preferred positive member.

A selected reference can label its members `+1` and `-1`.

The torsor exists before that naming.

This is the right language when structure determines an orientation pair but does not canonically select one member.

---

## Global reversal

**Status: D/P in several constructions**

Global reversal is simultaneous exchange of both members of an orientation torsor across an entire compatible domain.

If a native comparison has exactly two globally sign-related realizations, they represent the same unoriented structure.

In Born/EPR readout language:

    (a,b) -> (-a,-b)

preserves product parity.

Global reversal is therefore often gauge-like presentation freedom rather than a new physical outcome.

---

## Reconciliation criterion

**Status: P**

Suppose two honest `C2` sign-cocycle actions are defined on a common nonempty transitive incidence domain.

An equivariant comparison exists iff the two endpoint sign characters agree on the isotropy subgroup of one incidence.

When it exists, there are exactly two comparisons, differing by global reversal.

For multiple orbits, the criterion applies orbit by orbit.

This theorem removes the demand for absolute sign while preserving the demand for a real common domain and compatible lifted actions.

Reference selection cannot rescue isotropy mismatch.

---

## Mode

**Status: D/P on the current event carrier**

Mode describes registered orientation organization, not scalar amount.

In the current event algebra it is associated with a registered `S3` orientation-parity structure satisfying the declared inversion relation

    M_evt C_evt M_evt = C_evt^{-1}.

Mode answers a question of the form:

> In what orientation / relational organization is the event presented?

Mode is not relational complexity.

Mode is not automatically a reciprocal WXYZTI role.

Mode is not probability.

Mode is not time.

---

## Phase

**Status: D/C**

Phase describes position or orientation within a declared cyclic, harmonic, or registered-history structure.

Phase answers:

> Where within the declared relational cycle or harmonic organization is the state / history?

Phase is carrier-typed.

Examples include `C5`, `C10`, and registered `C20` phase constructions.

Guardrail:

    same phase arithmetic
    !=
    same state-action carrier.

Phase is not relational complexity and is not automatically physical phase measured in radians unless a correspondence is supplied.

---

## Sheet

**Status: D**

A sheet is a distinguished relational, spectral, quotient, receipt, or deck presentation of a finite carrier.
