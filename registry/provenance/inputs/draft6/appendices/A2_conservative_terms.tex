\subsection*{Additional Draft 5 terminology}
\noindent\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}p{.28\textwidth}X@{}}
\toprule
Term & Declared meaning and boundary \\
\midrule
Conservative informative action & An admitted recoverable action with a registered effect that preserves an additional specified invariant. \\
$\mathsf G$; $I_{\mathsf G}$ & Fixed positive-definite Hermitian form and its quadratic readout; neither the scalar encoding base $G$ nor a graph $G_n$. \\
$\mathsf A$; $U$ & A represented infinitesimal generator and finite propagator; their native admission is a separate requirement. \\
$\mathbf n$; $Q$ & Unit registered axis and full oriented frame. In the momentum example, $\mathbf n=\mathbf L/\|\mathbf L\|$, not generally the body axis. \\
Axis stabilizer & Rotations leaving the chosen oriented axis fixed. It is $SO(2)$ in the supplied rotor model, not a universal QR phase group. \\
Axial phase & A coordinate on the rotor's phase fiber relative to a local transverse-frame convention. It is not the finite event phase by definition. \\
$\mathsf A_M$; $\mathscr C$ & Vacuum Maxwell generator and curl on a specified field domain. They are not operators already extracted from WXYZTI. \\
$\mathcal E_{\rm EM}$; $\mathbf S_P$ & Conventional electromagnetic energy and Poynting flux, distinct from $\mathcal C$, scalar relay, and operational information current. \\
$\mathsf J$ & Signed electric--magnetic duality generator, with $\mathsf J^2=-I$. It is not charge current $\mathbf J$ or the WXYZTI involution $R_W$. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
