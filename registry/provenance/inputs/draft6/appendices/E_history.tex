\section{Registered-History Algebra}
\label{app:history}
\subsection{Typed histories}
Let $\mathscr C$ be a category whose objects are complete interface contexts and whose generating arrows are admitted events. A history is a composable word of generating arrows. Declared relations may identify some words, but an endpoint projection should not erase a retained word distinction unless the chosen history representation explicitly makes that quotient.

If inverses of admitted maps are available on their images, one can examine a corresponding partial groupoid or a groupoid completion. This algebraic inverse need not be an allowed forward physical execution. For example, resetting a record may require control of the complementary system. Formal reversal and experimentally available reversal have different admission requirements.

\subsection{Winding on the certified domain}
The imported Project41 registered-history construction reports a rank-three first-homology register with primitive species $r_0,r_1,r_2$, and a declared $G_{30}$ visibility test selecting $r_1$ \cite[Sec.~17]{cave2026becoming}. On this register the temporal readout is the integer-valued homomorphism
\[
\phi_1(r_0)=0,\qquad\phi_1(r_1)=1,\qquad\phi_1(r_2)=0,
\qquad\tth(\gamma)=\phi_1([\gamma]).
\]
This formulation makes the coordinate choice precise. Naming a primitive class in an arbitrary rank-three abelian group would not, without a complementary register or dual functional, uniquely define a coefficient-extraction map. The certified register supplies the needed structure.

The homology class of a concatenation of compatible closed histories is the sum of their classes, and reversal changes its sign. The additivity and oddness of $\tth$ therefore follow. The native part of the theorem is the construction and selection of the register, not the elementary algebra of a homomorphism once that register is supplied.

\subsection{Open-history extensions}
For an open history $\gamma:x\to y$, choose reference paths $p_x:o\to x$ and $p_y:o\to y$ in a suitable completion. One possible relative readout is
\[
\widetilde\tau(\gamma)=\tth(p_y^{-1}\circ\gamma\circ p_x).
\]
Its endpoint-dependent reference choices are additional data. Different choices can shift open-path values while leaving closed-loop winding unchanged. An edge cocycle representing the dual class gives another formulation. Neither extension is asserted to be the unique native one in this draft.

\subsection{Positive depth and signed winding}
Word length counts realized elementary occurrences. A signed winding counts their net homological contribution. A forward word can have zero winding; a reversed loop can have negative winding but positive execution cost. Consequently $|\gamma|_{\mathrm{event}}$ and $\tth(\gamma)$ cannot be exchanged without changing mathematical type and domain. The unit causal normalization uses the former.
