\subsection{Finite conservative updates and source selection}
Draft~5 refines the target without changing the preceding structural hypotheses. On a stated finite representation seek an actually admitted update $U$ and a source-selected positive form $\mathsf G_f$ such that
\[
U^\dagger\mathsf G_fU=\mathsf G_f.
\]
The identity must hold on the claimed domain; recovery, informative effect, and locality must also hold. Group averaging produces invariant metrics for arbitrary finite group representations, so the existence of some form is not itself native physical evidence. A selector must explain why this form, update, and observable belong to the same construction before any comparison data are used.

\subsection{The gyroscopic stabilizer target}
Specify a group $\mathcal G$ acting on complete registered frames, an observable orbit, and its stabilizer $H$. Test the orbit map $\mathcal G/H$ and the retained fiber action on the same native domain. A point stabilizer, a normal character kernel, and a free deck group are different action data. The conventional model $SO(2)\hookrightarrow SO(3)\to S^2$ motivates this test; it is not its native answer. A continuous limit also needs a sequence or representation extension that actually supports arbitrarily small admitted reorientations.

\subsection{A discrete Maxwell constraint-and-evolution target}
A candidate incidence complex must provide maps
\[
C^0\xrightarrow{D_0}C^1\xrightarrow{D_1}C^2\xrightarrow{D_2}C^3,
\qquad D_1D_0=0,\quad D_2D_1=0,
\]
with declared meanings for field cochains, sources, boundaries, and constitutive pairings. For positive matrices $M_e,M_b$, one reference algebra is
\[
M_e\dot e=D_1^{\mathsf T}M_b b-j,\qquad \dot b=-D_1e,
\qquad D_0^{\mathsf T}M_e e=\rho,\quad D_2b=0.
\]
It preserves the source-free quadratic energy and propagates the constraints when $\dot\rho+D_0^{\mathsf T}j=0$. Appendix~\ref{app:conservativecalculations} proves these conditional identities. The existence of such generic incidence algebra is not a construction of these objects from the Thalean graph, and adjacency alone does not select a cell complex or constitutive matrices.

\subsection{Common-domain and continuum gates}
A successful finite model must identify the native WXYZTI operations, any modal reflection, and the state/constraint maps without overriding their existing action laws. A proposed continuum sequence must state spatial and event scales, embeddings or coarse-grainings, a convergence norm, and the observables and regimes in which error tends to zero. Exact finite conservation alone does not establish dispersion, polarization, Lorentz symmetry, a physical speed, or an information-current encoding. These are separate gates, not consequences of a favorable dimension count.
