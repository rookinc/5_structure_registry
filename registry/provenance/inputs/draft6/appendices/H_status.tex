\subsection{Status vocabulary}
\textbf{D} means a definition or explicit model requirement. \textbf{P} means a proposition proved in this manuscript. \textbf{C} means a concrete finite construction or exact algebra checked by the included script. \textbf{I} means an imported finite result whose full upstream proof or audit is not rerun here. \textbf{H} means a working hypothesis. \textbf{F} means a physical correspondence or limit still to be established. In the Draft~5 additions, standard mechanics and electromagnetism are established comparison theories; F labels their proposed native QR correspondence, not their conventional validity. P does not claim novelty, and C labels only the stated executed construction. These categories do not form a single ladder: an imported theorem can be mathematically rigorous while remaining independent of a physical interpretation.
