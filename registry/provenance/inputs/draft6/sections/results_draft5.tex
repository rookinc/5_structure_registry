\section*{Draft 5: conservative informative dynamics}
\begin{principle}
\centering
\textbf{Relational complexity is the subject.}\\[2pt]
Information conservation is the constraint.\\[2pt]
Admissible transduction is the mechanism.\\[5pt]
\textbf{Conservation constrains change; it does not prohibit change.}
\end{principle}
This draft adds a conservative dynamical subclass without replacing complete recoverability by preservation of a scalar. In a supplied linear representation, a fixed positive-definite Hermitian form $\mathsf G$ gives
\[
I_{\mathsf G}(X)=X^\dagger\mathsf G X,\qquad
\dot X=\mathsf A X,\qquad
\boxed{\mathsf A^\dagger\mathsf G+\mathsf G\mathsf A=0}.
\]
The boxed condition preserves the declared form for every state. It does not select the form, determine the generator, or identify $I_{\mathsf G}$ with relational complexity $\mathcal C$.
