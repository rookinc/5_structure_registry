\section*{Scope, Evidence, and Notation}
A definition fixes the model vocabulary; a proposition follows from stated hypotheses; a checked construction records what the included code executes; an imported result retains its upstream provenance. Physical correspondence requires a specified observable, instrument, and, where relevant, a controlled limit. Appendix~H records these roles as D, P, C, I, H, and F.

Quantum Relativity names this research program, independently of Finkelstein's earlier use of the phrase \citep{finkelstein1996}. Draft~6 retains relational complexity under information-preserving transduction as its thesis and adds selected reference and incidence-based orientation reconciliation. The retained finite carrier, cover, kernel, history, and modal results constrain this program; they do not select a universal $\mathcal C$. The naming background is confined to the Introduction.
