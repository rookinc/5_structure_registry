## New main-text structure

**Section 6B: Conservative informative action.** An already admitted, recoverable,
and informative action may additionally preserve a specified invariant. Recovery,
fixed-readout conservation, and informative effect are separate tests. The
identity, an invertible stretch, and a norm-preserving folding illustrate the
separations.

**Section 37A: Invariant forms and generators.** Proposition 37A.1 proves that a
continuous finite-dimensional linear flow preserves a fixed positive Hermitian
form for every initial state exactly when A^dagger G + G A = 0. The text gives
finite propagators, constant-generator exponentials, Cayley steps, the nonlinear
level-set condition, the extra dot(G) term for moving metrics, and the source-work
term. The form, representation, and admissible generator must be supplied; the
criterion does not select them.

**Section 37B: Gyroscopic precession and phase fiber.** Proposition 37B.1 proves
unit-axis tangency and the cross-product kernel/image decomposition. The full
frame maps to an oriented axis through SO(2) -> SO(3) -> S^2. The stabilizer is
not normal, the base is not a quotient group, and the frame bundle is not a global
product. In a local Euler chart the axial angular velocity is
phi_dot + varphi_dot cos(theta), not simply the derivative of a global extra
phase coordinate. Torque is an imported mechanical law. Its parallel component
changes momentum magnitude; its transverse component changes the momentum axis.
The slow, steady heavy-top formula is presented as an approximation, not a law
for a general nutating top.

**Section 37C: Maxwell translation.** Divergence equations specify constraints;
curl equations specify reciprocal evolution; charge continuity preserves
compatible constraints. On an explicitly periodic field domain the curl
realization is self-adjoint and the coupled six-component evolution is
skew-adjoint. The conserved quadratic form is total electromagnetic energy,
not a pointwise invariant or QR complexity. Poynting's local balance retains
boundary flux and work on sources. Constant source-free duality rotations are
separate from spatial rotations, and need a duality-compatible boundary domain.

**Section 37D: Native targets and failure tests.** Invariant group-averaged metrics
exist too generally to select a Thalean physical mechanism. A fixed finite group
has no nonconstant continuous path through identity. A phase-fiber candidate
requires an explicit orbit, stabilizer, retained full frame, and admitted base
action. WXYZTI's commuting C3/reciprocal pair and native Mode's inversion action
remain inequivalent under the proposed generator identification. A native Maxwell
bridge must construct incidence, sources, constitutive pairings, signed evolution,
constraint preservation, and locality on one admitted domain.

## Corrections to preliminary discussion

These are qualifications of the conversation and approved plan, not alterations
to the retained finite theorems.

1. Information conservation does not force a Euclidean metric, a cross product,
   linearity, or SO(3). Norm-preserving orientation motion is tangent; a constant
   generator and uniform cone require additional hypotheses. Rotational covariance
   of a nonlinear field with transformed parameters does not make it an isometry.
2. The instantaneous momentum-axis change is **along** transverse torque. The
   cross-product generator is perpendicular to momentum and that change. The
   familiar right-angle observation must not reverse these two roles.
3. The axial kernel is invisible to the axis register. Its physical phase
   interpretation needs a full frame or marked transverse direction and a local
   comparison convention. It is not the native C3 phase by definition.
4. SO(3)/SO(2) is a homogeneous space, not a group quotient by a normal kernel.
   It is not the same construction as D8/Z(D8). An unoriented line instead has
   an embedded O(2) stabilizer and RP2 base.
5. A body's symmetry axis and its angular-momentum axis need not coincide.
   Appendix N retains transverse inertia in the exact regular-top equation and
   identifies the slow-branch approximation and vertical degeneracy.
6. Maxwell skew-adjointness is an operator-domain result, not a conclusion from
   the visual block pattern alone. A vanishing surface flux for one solution is
   not sufficient. Periodicity here is a mathematical comparison choice, not a
   cosmological topology claim.
7. Electromagnetic energy, charge current, energy flux, and operational information
   current are different quantities. Prescribed sources do not automatically
   supply a closed, recoverable field-plus-matter model.
8. Maxwell duality is generated by a signed quarter-turn with square -I. It is
   not WXYZTI's unsigned involution or native Mode. Boundary conditions can
   break duality even in a source-free region.
9. An exact finite matrix step is not automatically an admitted finite-state
   Thalean event. Arbitrary invariant metrics and smooth interpolations do not
   establish source selection or a controlled continuum limit.
10. Neither angular-momentum magnitude nor electromagnetic energy is identified
    with C(R); no native law dot(C)=0 is inferred from these examples. The datum
    c_Th=1 does not supply SI c or an electromagnetic constitutive calibration.
