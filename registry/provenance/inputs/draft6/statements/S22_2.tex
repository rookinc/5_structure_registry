\begin{proposition}[Unit causal bound]
Suppose every primitive step in an admitted history changes field address by distance at most one. Then every such history of depth $n>0$ satisfies
\input{equations/E30}
If the domain contains at least one event-preserving adjacent handoff of irreducible depth one, then $\cth=1$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Let $v_0,\ldots,v_n$ be the addresses encountered. The triangle inequality gives
$d(v_0,v_n)\leq\sum_{k=1}^{n}d(v_{k-1},v_k)\leq n$.
The stated one-step handoff has ratio one and supplies the reverse inequality for the supremum.
\end{proof}
