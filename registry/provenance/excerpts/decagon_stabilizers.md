The twenty-four registered ten-cycles form one Aut(G60) orbit. Each cycle has setwise stabilizer of order twenty, written D10 in this source: ten rotations and ten reflections acting faithfully on the decagon. The twelve answering pairs are pairs of lifted ten-cycles above a quotient pentagon. Their setwise stabilizers have order forty and are D10 x C2. The extra C2 exchanges the two lifted cycles. No equality of this D10 with an order-ten group is intended by the source's subscript convention.
