## What was not done

This is not an exhaustive account-wide conversation export, entire Library enumeration, or whole-repository sweep. Targeted Library results can miss additional occurrences. The current conversation is represented by a labeled digest, not a fabricated complete transcript file. No external literature was used to fill a missing native identity.

No historical mathematical producer was executed. Stored PASS flags report earlier work; this archive's validator checks files, source anchors, and reference integrity only. It does not certify the mathematical claims inside each source.

The recent full history/pentagon action and cocycle-cancellation claims remain conversation-reported until their original action tables are preserved and verified. The known history Gram/Clifford matrices do not independently establish those full-action claims. The G9000 current-to-history intertwiner and native status of the frame multiplier likewise remain open at the recorded boundary.

## Matching is lexical, not semantic

The scanner preserves case, leading zeros, original spelling, file/line/column and exponent placement. It distinguishes a subscript from a superscript and parentheses, keeping `U_2` separate from `U(2)` at the lexical layer. Some source prose also uses U2 for U(2); that spelling correctly has multiple candidates rather than a global rule.

Direct matches, mathematical font-wrapped subscripts, common multi-letter forms, parenthesized matrix-group names, and selected Unicode subscripts are recognized. The matcher is not a complete LaTeX parser and does not expand arbitrary user macros. Equation tags, source paths and serialization false positives are retained in explicit metadata bins.

An occurrence with one candidate is a lookup suggestion, not a verified statement of identity. Multiple candidates are an instruction to inspect local context. Full semantic disambiguation of every one of the 6,679 matches has not been claimed. Missing group instances remain unknown; they are never guessed from a subscript.
